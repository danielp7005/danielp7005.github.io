Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Dashspace LLC ("Dashspace," "we," "us," or "our") provides the MyTravelQ mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains what information MyTravelQ collects, how it is used and stored, and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

MyTravelQ may collect and store information that you provide when using the app, including:

  • Account information: such as your email address and information needed to create and authenticate your account.
  • Trip information: destinations, countries or regions, preferred travel months, trip length, flight-length category, activities, scheduled month and year, notes, and other travel-planning information you choose to enter.
  • Profile information: basic account or profile data associated with your MyTravelQ account.
  • Technical information: limited technical information may be processed by the service providers used to operate the app, such as device, network, security, authentication, or diagnostic information necessary to provide and protect the service.

How we use information

We use information collected through MyTravelQ to:

  • create and authenticate user accounts;
  • store, display, organize, and synchronize your saved trips and timeline;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the app;
  • respond to support, privacy, or account-related requests; and
  • improve the reliability and functionality of MyTravelQ.

Service providers

MyTravelQ uses third-party infrastructure and services to provide app functionality. The current version uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, for account authentication and cloud data storage. These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. MyTravelQ is not designed to share personal information with advertising networks or data brokers, and the current version does not use user data for cross-app or cross-site advertising tracking.

Data storage and security

Account and trip information may be stored using cloud infrastructure provided by Google Firebase. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature of the app and the information it handles. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data retention

We retain account and trip information while your account remains active or for as long as reasonably necessary to provide MyTravelQ. Information may also be retained for a limited period where necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or backup and recovery processes.

Account and data deletion

If you create a MyTravelQ account, you may initiate account deletion from within the app. Account deletion is intended to remove the account and associated MyTravelQ data that we are not legally required to retain.

If you are unable to use the in-app deletion feature, you may contact us at contact@dashspace.io for assistance with an account or data-deletion request.

Your choices

You may choose what trip information you add to MyTravelQ. You may edit or delete trip information through the app. You may also request assistance regarding access, correction, or deletion of account-related information by contacting us.

Children's privacy

MyTravelQ is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that information from a child under 13 has been collected in a manner inconsistent with applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

International users

MyTravelQ may use service providers that process or store information in locations outside your state, province, or country. By using the app, your information may therefore be processed in jurisdictions with data-protection rules that differ from those where you live.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as MyTravelQ changes. When we make material changes, we will update the date shown at the top of this page and provide any additional notice required by applicable law.
