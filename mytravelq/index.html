HOW IT WORKS

A simple three-step flow.

MyTravelQ is designed to help you move from inspiration to an actual travel plan without unnecessary complexity.

STEP 1

Build your wishlist

Save interesting destinations and capture the details that matter to you, from best travel months to trip type.

STEP 2

Compare what fits next

Filter and review your saved trips to decide what works best for the season, your schedule, or your travel style.

STEP 3

Place it on your timeline

Assign a month and year so your ideas become a visible future plan you can keep building over time.
