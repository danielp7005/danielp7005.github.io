How a project runs

Same four phases, every time.

  1. Phase 01

    Discovery

    Two weeks to define the one job the app has to do, and the one metric that proves it's working.

  2. Phase 02

    Design

    Working screens in your hands within days, not a deck of concepts. We design in the medium people will actually use.

  3. Phase 03

    Build

    Weekly demos on a real device or a real URL. No "trust us, it's almost done" — you see it running throughout.

  4. Phase 04

    Launch & hold

    We ship it, watch the dashboard with you for the first month, and hand over something your own team can maintain.
